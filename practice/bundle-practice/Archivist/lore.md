# The Tales of Dunk and Egg

## The Tourney at Ashford Meadow
Ser Duncan the Tall, often simply called Dunk, traveled to Ashford Meadow to participate in the great tourney. Despite his lack of formal knightly pedigree, his immense size and strength made him a formidable opponent. It was here he met a young, bald boy named Egg, who offered to be his squire. The tourney was marred by a Trial of Seven, a rare judicial combat where Dunk had to fight alongside six others to defend his innocence against Prince Aerion Targaryen.

## The Sworn Sword
After Ashford, Ser Duncan and Egg traveled to the Reach. They entered the service of Ser Eustace Osgrey of Standfast, a proud but impoverished knight. They soon found themselves embroiled in a bitter dispute over a dam built by Lady Rohanne Webber of Coldmoat, which was drying up Ser Eustace's lands.
