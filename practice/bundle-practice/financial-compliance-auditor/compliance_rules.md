# Firm Trading Policies

## Insider Trading & Blackout Periods
No employee may trade company stock within 14 days prior to a quarterly earnings report. Any trades executed during this blackout period will result in immediate termination and reporting to the SEC.

## Cryptocurrency Exposure
Employees are permitted to hold spot cryptocurrencies. However, trading cryptocurrency derivatives (options, futures, or leveraged tokens) on company equipment or using company funds is strictly prohibited.
