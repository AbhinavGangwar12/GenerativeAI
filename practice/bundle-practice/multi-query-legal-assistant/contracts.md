# Standard Executive Employment Agreement

## Article 4: Confidentiality and Intellectual Property
The Executive agrees that all inventions, algorithms, and trade secrets developed during company time or using company equipment remain the sole property of TechNova Inc. Post-employment non-disclosure obligations shall persist for a period of 5 years.

## Article 7: Moonlighting and Outside Activities
During the term of employment, the Executive shall devote their full business energies to the Company. Engaging in outside business ventures, consulting, or serving on external boards is strictly prohibited without prior written consent from the Board of Directors. 

## Article 9: Termination for Cause
The Company reserves the right to immediately sever this agreement for "Cause", defined as: (a) embezzlement or fraud; (b) willful violation of Article 4 or Article 7; (c) conviction of a felony. Upon termination for cause, all unvested equity is immediately forfeit.
