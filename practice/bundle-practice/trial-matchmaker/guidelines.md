# General Clinical Trial Guidelines

## Phase 3 Trials
Phase 3 trials compare a new treatment to the standard of care. Patients must strictly meet the inclusion criteria. If a patient has a history of severe cardiac events (e.g., heart attack within the last 5 years), they are automatically disqualified from Phase 3 metabolic trials.

## Informed Consent
All patients matched to a trial must complete the Form-88C Informed Consent prior to unblinding or receiving experimental dosages.
