Ser Duncan the Tall was a hedge knight who traveled the Seven Kingdoms. 
He was accompanied by his squire, Egg, who was secretly Aegon V Targaryen. 
Together, they competed in the Tourney at Ashford Meadow, where Dunk defended the innocent 
and made powerful enemies. Years later, Dunk would rise to become the Lord Commander 
of the Kingsguard, serving King Aegon.
