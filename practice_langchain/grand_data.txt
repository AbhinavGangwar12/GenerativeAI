At the Tourney of the Blackwood, Ser Duncan the Tall rode with a shield painted with a shooting star. In the first tilt, he unhorsed a seasoned knight of House Bracken. In the second tilt, he narrowly defeated a mystery knight. Egg, his squire, managed the horses and placed wagers on Dunk's victories using the meager silver they had left. The tourney was overseen by Lord Bloodraven.
